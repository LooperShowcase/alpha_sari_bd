Novitec shows the finished N-Largo version of the Ferrari 12Cilindri, a carbon-fibre widebody that adds 7 cm of width at the front and 13 cm at the rear. The German tuner builds it as a limited edition, developed with designer Vittorio Strosek, and gives no output for the 6.5-litre V12, which makes 830 hp as standard. The build number is still missing too.

Ferrari N.V. (NYSE: RACE) trades at approximately $411.13 per share, while its newly unveiled all-electric supercar, the Ferrari Luce, commands a starting price of $640,000 and is already sold out through 2027.
